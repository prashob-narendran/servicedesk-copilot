# SOP – Hardware and Device Requests

## New device requests

New laptops and monitors for managed customers require approval from the customer's IT manager. Standard devices from the catalogue are delivered within 5 business days of approval; non-standard devices take up to 15 business days.

## Broken or damaged devices

1. Collect the device serial number, a description of the damage and a photo if possible.
2. Check warranty status in the asset system. In-warranty faults follow the Returns and Exchanges policy.
3. Accidental damage (for example a cracked screen or liquid damage) is not covered by the standard warranty. The customer receives a repair quote within 2 business days.
4. Offer a loan laptop if the user cannot work; loan devices are available for up to 10 business days.

## Printers and peripherals

For printer problems, ask the user to restart the printer and check for paper jams and toner levels. Network printers that are offline for all users should be escalated to IT Support Level 2.
