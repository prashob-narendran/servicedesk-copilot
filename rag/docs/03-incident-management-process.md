# TechServ Incident Management Process

An incident is an unplanned interruption or reduction in quality of a service. The goal is to restore normal service as fast as possible and minimise business impact.

## Stages

1. **Log:** every incident is recorded as a ticket with the reporter, affected service, symptoms and time first noticed.
2. **Categorise and prioritise:** the ticket is assigned a queue and a priority (P1–P4) using the SLA policy. The AI copilot suggests both; the agent confirms.
3. **Investigate and diagnose:** the agent checks known errors, recent changes and similar past tickets. The copilot's knowledge-base search shows matching procedures and past resolutions.
4. **Resolve and recover:** apply the fix or workaround and confirm with the customer that service is restored.
5. **Close:** record the resolution and the root cause if known. Tickets are closed automatically 3 business days after resolution if the customer does not reply.

## Major incidents

A major incident is any P1 that affects more than one customer, or any confirmed security breach.

- The shift lead becomes the incident manager and opens a bridge call within 15 minutes.
- Customers are updated every 30 minutes on the status page and by email.
- Technical Support and Service Outages and Maintenance teams join the bridge; Security joins for breaches.
- A post-incident review (PIR) is held within 5 business days. The PIR report covers the timeline, root cause, impact and corrective actions, and is shared with affected customers.

## Problem management

If the same incident recurs three or more times in 30 days, a problem record is opened to find and remove the root cause.
