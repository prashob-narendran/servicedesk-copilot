# TechServ Billing, Refunds and Payments Policy

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the 1st business day of each month and are payable within 30 days. Customers can download invoices from the billing portal. Requests to change the billing address or tax details are handled by Billing and Payments within 2 business days.

## Duplicate charges

If a customer reports a duplicate charge, the agent checks the payment gateway for two successful transactions with the same amount and date. Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 business days of confirmation. The customer receives a refund reference number by email.

## Refunds

- Annual subscriptions can be cancelled for a full refund within 14 days of purchase or renewal.
- After 14 days, annual subscriptions are refunded pro rata for unused full months, minus a 10% administration fee.
- Monthly subscriptions are not refunded; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.
- Professional services already delivered are not refundable.

Refunds above 5,000 USD need approval from the finance manager, which adds up to 3 business days.

## Failed payments

After a failed card payment, the system retries after 3 and 7 days. The account is suspended if payment has not succeeded 14 days after the due date. Suspended accounts are restored within 1 hour of a successful payment.
