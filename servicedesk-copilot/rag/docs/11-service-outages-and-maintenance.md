# TechServ Service Outages and Planned Maintenance

## Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance takes place on Saturdays between 22:00 and 02:00 Gulf Standard Time. Customers are notified at least 5 business days in advance by email and on the status page. Emergency maintenance for security patches can be scheduled with 24 hours' notice.

## Status page

The public status page shows the current state of every TechServ service, ongoing incidents and planned maintenance. Customers can subscribe to email or SMS updates. During an outage, agents should point customers to the status page and link their ticket to the master incident instead of troubleshooting each ticket separately.

## Reporting an outage

When several customers report the same symptom within a short time, the agent:

1. Checks the status page and monitoring dashboards.
2. Raises a P1 ticket in the Service Outages and Maintenance queue if no master incident exists.
3. Links all related customer tickets to the master incident.

## Data backups

Customer databases on managed hosting are backed up every 4 hours, and backups are kept for 30 days. A restore from backup can be requested through Technical Support; standard restores complete within 8 hours.
