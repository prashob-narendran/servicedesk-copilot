# TechServ Service Catalogue and Support Queues

TechServ Ltd is a fictional managed IT and software services company used for this project. This document lists the support queues, what each queue handles and how tickets are routed.

## How routing works

Every incoming ticket is assigned to exactly one queue. The AI copilot suggests the queue; the service-desk agent confirms it. Tickets whose suggested queue has confidence below 50% are flagged for human review and must be routed manually by the shift lead. A ticket that is moved between queues more than twice is escalated to the shift lead.

## Queues

### Technical Support
Handles faults in software and infrastructure used by customers: application errors, integration failures, performance problems, configuration issues and bugs. Example: "the API returns error 500 after the update".

### Product Support
Handles questions and problems about how TechServ products work: feature behaviour, settings, licensing questions about a feature, and product documentation. Example: "how do I export reports to Excel?".

### IT Support
Handles internal end-user IT for managed customers: laptops, accounts, passwords, VPN, email, printers and Microsoft 365. Example: "my account is locked".

### Customer Service
Handles general account administration, contract questions, complaints and feedback that are not about a technical fault.

### Billing and Payments
Handles invoices, duplicate charges, payment failures, refunds and changes to billing details.

### Returns and Exchanges
Handles returns of hardware purchased through TechServ, replacement of faulty devices and exchanges within the return window.

### Service Outages and Maintenance
Handles reports of service-wide outages, degraded performance affecting many users, and questions about planned maintenance.

### Sales and Pre-Sales
Handles quotes, new licences, upgrades, demos and pricing questions from prospects and existing customers.

### Human Resources
Handles HR-related requests from managed-service customers who use TechServ's HR platform: payroll portal access, leave records and employee onboarding records.

### General Inquiry
Handles anything that does not fit another queue. Agents in this queue re-route the ticket within 1 business hour once the topic is clear.
