# TechServ Hardware Returns and Exchanges Policy

Applies to hardware (laptops, monitors, docking stations, headsets) bought through TechServ.

## Return window

Unused hardware in its original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened but undamaged items can be returned within 30 days with a 15% restocking fee.

## Faulty devices

Devices that fail within the 12-month warranty are replaced free of charge. The customer must provide the serial number and a description of the fault. Replacement devices are shipped within 3 business days after the faulty device is received, or immediately for customers on the Premium support plan (advance replacement).

## How to start a return

1. The customer opens a ticket with the order number and serial number.
2. Returns and Exchanges issues a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number within 1 business day.
3. The customer ships the device with the RMA number using the prepaid label.

Devices must be wiped of personal data before return; TechServ also performs a certified data wipe on every returned device.
