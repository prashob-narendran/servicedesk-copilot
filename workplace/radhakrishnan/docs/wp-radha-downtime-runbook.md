# Workplace Runbook – Digital Banking Downtime

Illustrative runbook written for the Situated Learning section; it contains no confidential content.

## Declaring an incident

If the call centre receives more than 10 calls about the same digital-banking failure (mobile app, online banking, card payments) within 15 minutes, the call-centre lead raises a high-priority IT incident and pages the IT on-call manager. Do not open a separate ticket for every call: link calls to the master incident.

## Customer communication

1. Within 15 minutes, IT and the call-centre lead agree a short customer message.
2. The message is played on the phone line (IVR) and posted on the bank's website and app status banner.
3. Agents tell customers that branch and ATM services are still available, if that is true, and never give an estimated fix time unless IT has confirmed it.

## Updates and closure

IT updates the call centre every 30 minutes until service is restored. After restoration, the incident manager confirms with the call centre that call volumes are back to normal before closing the incident. A post-incident review is held within 5 business days for any outage longer than 1 hour.
