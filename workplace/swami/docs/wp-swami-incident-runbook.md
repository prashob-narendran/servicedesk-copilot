# Workplace Runbook – Platform Incidents and Client SLAs

Illustrative runbook written for the Situated Learning section; it contains no confidential content.

## Case priorities for enterprise clients

| Priority | Definition | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Platform down or a whole client unable to use it | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | Major feature broken for many users, workaround exists | 2 hours | 1 business day |
| P3 | Single user or minor feature affected | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| P4 | Question, feedback or how-to request | 2 business days | 10 business days |

## When the learning platform is down for a client

1. Check the cloud provider's health dashboard and our monitoring alerts.
2. Raise a P1 case in Salesforce and link all related client cases to it.
3. Post a notice on the platform status page within 30 minutes and email the client's main contact.
4. If a live training or certification session is affected, offer to reschedule it without charge.
5. Update the client every hour until service is restored, and send a short incident report within 3 business days.

## Video playback problems

If videos do not load in one browser but work in another, ask the learner to disable browser extensions and allow third-party cookies for the video player domain, then test again. Collect the browser version and a screenshot of the browser console for Level 2.
